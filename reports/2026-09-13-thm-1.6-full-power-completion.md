Implementation head `8a64e30622178cb294d8d89f0ba60d003619fcef` passed all four PR workflows, including native builds on Linux, Windows and macOS. Subsequent review fixes and release metadata require fresh head validation; those earlier runs are not substituted for final-head acceptance.

Default campaigns remain bounded; no full datasets, generated answers, judge calls or hardware performance campaign ran here. The machine-readable ledger carries the 71-provider and 42-physical-family census.

## Boundaries

T0–T3 remain the only logical tiers. Compute, physical storage and logical memory remain orthogonal. Source bytes and scope are authoritative. CE L0–L6 stays independent and optional. Privileged actuation and tool speculation default off; explicit gates and rollback apply. A real-runtime provider owns its KV allocations. Simulated A0–A11 timing cannot establish task success, and unknown TUFR stays null.

`archive/v1.5.0-stable` stays at `de26865f36df2205c29a470e51c65d5bf9beca4e`. A 1.6 archive may only pin the accepted normal merge after post-merge main CI succeeds. Closeout descendants do not move that archive.
