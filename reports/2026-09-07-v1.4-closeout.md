## Accepted 1.4 surface

1. Strict shadow telemetry for resident hits/misses, hard misses, planned retrieval, stale-resident failures and bounded prefetch.
2. Raw misses and `avoidable=true` residency misses are distinct; only the latter can create resident-capacity benefit.
3. All observed tasks and explicit demand tasks are separate denominators; per-item minimum-demand gates are enforced.
4. A strict non-authoritative measurement catalog can add resident/miss-cost estimates, safe locators and scope labels but cannot override canonical THM state.
5. T1 warm-directory projection is locator-only; display topics derive from safe locator stems rather than historical source-prefix keys.
6. Shadow T0 residency recommendation compares avoidable-miss token benefit against repeated resident carry cost and uses bounded exact 0/1 packing for that finite token objective.
7. Bounded co-demand prefetch requires current canonical seeds; prefetch outcomes cannot train their own future demand signal.
8. Bounded resident-budget feedback uses avoidable miss pressure, context pressure, prefetch pollution and stale-resident risk, and never mutates the actual budget.
9. Hermes has an opt-in T1 locator snapshot with budget 0 by default, verified in-profile file targets, and session-boundary-only refresh semantics.

## What “stable” does and does not mean

Stable here means the public 1.4 implementation, its read-only/advisory boundaries, provider integration and repository checks reached a versioned recovery point on the accepted SHA.

It does **not** mean:

- a learned or universally optimal residency policy exists;
- automatic T0–T3 movement is enabled;
- automatic Hermes/T0 budget mutation is enabled;
- a locator is evidence;
- retrieval/mention/prefetch is causal proof of usefulness;
- the adaptive policy has beaten the fixed-budget/activity baseline on held-out real tasks.

Automatic policy mutation remains gated on runtime/task A/B evidence that measures task quality together with avoidable miss/reacquisition cost, latency, stale-state failures and prefetch waste.
